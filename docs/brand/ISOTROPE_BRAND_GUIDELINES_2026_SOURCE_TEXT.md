BRAND GUIDELINES ISOTROPE 
ISOTROPE brings specialist knowledge to hard-to-reach places. Our team combines practical skills, rope 
access and technology to inspect, repair and maintain structures, support construction projects and provide 
standby rescue. With clear communication, precision and a commitment to safety, we help clients 
understand what their structures need and make informed decisions. Whatever the challenge, our purpose 
stays the same: to bring the right knowledge and the care it takes to do the job well.

ISOTROPE combines purple tones with black and white to create a distinctive, consistent 
identity. Use the colour logo on white, black or deep purple backgrounds, selecting the 
wordmark and tagline colours for clear contrast. Single-colour black or white versions 
are available when colour reproduction is limited.
The logo and tagline can be used in vertical or horizontal layouts. The vertical version 
places the symbol above the name and the tagline below, creating a compact, centred 
composition. The horizontal version places the symbol, name and tagline side by side, 
making it suitable for wider spaces. Always preserve the proportions, spacing and 
alignment shown, and ensure the tagline remains legible.

BRAND GUIDELINES
TEXTURES
ISOTROPE 
These textures use close-ups and outlines of the symbol to bring 
ISOTROPE’s identity into emails, letterheads, notices and internal 
documents. Their subtle application creates consistency across 
everyday company communications, keeping the information as the 
main focus.
The first highlights the centre of the symbol, where different forces 
converge towards a shared solution, reflecting the coordination 
between ISOTROPE team, methods and technology. The second reveals 
a detail of its outline, highlighting the triangular geometry and the 
balance between its angles, cuts and negative spaces: a design that 
communicates precision and consistency even when shown in 
fragments.
Both are used with low contrast and generous open space to support 
readability and maintain a clean, professional presentation.
